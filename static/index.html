Is your website affected by PHP2Shell?

PHP2Shell (CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619) is a critical vulnerability in PHP that lets attackers run code on a web server. Enter your website's address to see which PHP version and applications it reveals, and whether that version is patched.

The check loads your start page the same way a browser does.

Abuse may be reported to the authorities.

How it works

We load the start page of your website and compare what it sends back with known fingerprints of PHP applications.

Rules of use

Only check websites you own or have permission to check. Abuse may be reported to the authorities.

Privacy

For every check we store the time, your IP address, your browser's name and the checked address, to prevent and investigate abuse. These records are deleted after 30 days, unless they are needed to report or investigate a specific case of misuse. No cookies, no tracking, no third-party content.
